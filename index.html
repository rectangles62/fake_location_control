Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Rectangles (“Rectangles,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the Fake Location Control Android application (the “App”). By installing or using the App, you acknowledge the practices described here.

1. Information the App accesses

The App requests access to precise and approximate device location so it can display the device’s current position, center the map, calculate location-related values, and provide the location-control features selected by the user. When the user enables mock-location functionality, the App may provide selected or generated location values to Android’s mock-location framework.

The App may also access device orientation and motion-related sensor information, including the Android rotation-vector sensor, when the floating joystick’s device-direction display is enabled. This information is used to calculate and display a direction indicator.

The App may request permission to display over other apps when the user enables the floating joystick. This permission allows the joystick overlay to appear above other applications. The overlay permission is optional and is not required for ordinary map use.

2. How information is used

Location, orientation, and sensor values are used to operate the App’s map, marker, joystick, randomization, direction, and mock-location features. Settings such as selected coordinates, signal values, randomization preferences, joystick preferences, and service state may be stored locally on the device so the App can restore its configuration and continue an intentionally enabled foreground service.

3. Information sharing and sale

Based on the current design, Rectangles does not sell personal information and does not operate a service that requires a user account. Location and sensor values are intended to be processed on the device and passed to Android’s location framework when the relevant feature is enabled. Rectangles does not intentionally receive a copy of those values through the App’s ordinary operation.

The App may open external pages, including its Google Play listing, when the user chooses an appropriate button. Those pages are operated by third parties and are governed by their own privacy policies. If advertising, analytics, crash reporting, cloud storage, or other third-party SDKs are added to the production build, this section and the disclosures below must be updated before publication to identify them accurately.

4. Permissions and user control

You can control permissions through Android settings. You may deny or revoke location, notification, and overlay permissions, and you can stop the mock-location or floating-joystick services from within the App or Android system controls. Revoking a required permission may prevent the related feature from working.

5. Retention and deletion

App preferences stored locally remain on the device until they are changed, reset, the App’s data is cleared, or the App is uninstalled. Because the current design does not require a Rectangles account or cloud profile, Rectangles does not provide a separate server-side account deletion process for ordinary App use. Android system logs and data created by other applications or services are controlled by Android and their respective operators.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Rectangles account or online form. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Rectangles, contact us at rectangles62@gmail.com.

7. Security

1. Information the App accesses and stores

The App requests precise and approximate device location so it can display the device’s current position, center the map, calculate location-related values, and provide the location-control features selected by you. When you enable mock-location functionality, the App may provide selected or generated location values to Android’s mock-location framework.

The App may request notification permission, foreground-service permissions, and permission to display over other apps when you enable the floating joystick. The overlay permission is optional and is not required for ordinary map use.

Settings such as selected coordinates, signal values, randomization preferences, joystick preferences, consent-related app state, and service state may be stored locally on the device. The App does not require a Rectangles account for ordinary use.

2. How the App uses location

Location is used to operate the App’s map, marker, joystick, randomization, direction, and mock-location features. Based on the current App design, these feature values are intended to be processed on the device and passed to Android’s location framework when the relevant feature is enabled. Rectangles does not intentionally receive a server-side copy of those feature values through the ordinary operation of the App.
